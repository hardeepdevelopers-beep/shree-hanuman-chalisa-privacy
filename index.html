This Privacy Policy explains how the Shri Hanuman Chalisa application (the “App”) handles information when you use its devotional features.

01Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, password, home address or precise location to use its devotional content.

The App does not ask you to upload personal photos or files as a condition of use. Features such as sharing or saving devotional images may involve images and apps that you choose on your device.

Third-party services used by the App may automatically process technical, usage, advertising or diagnostic information, as described below. This can include device identifiers and network information.

02Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve functionality, and Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and stability issues.

Depending on device settings and SDK behavior, these services may process app interactions, device and app information, crash logs, diagnostic data and identifiers. We do not require your name, email address or phone number to use the App.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

03Google AdMob Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers where available, IP addresses and ad interaction information, subject to applicable consent choices, device settings and Google's policies.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps and Google advertising practices.

04Data Sharing

We do not sell user account information. The App uses third-party providers for analytics, diagnostics, advertising and devotional content delivery. Those providers may process information needed to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

When you choose to share a devotional image or message, the destination app or service you select handles that content according to its own privacy practices. Sharing is initiated by you.

05Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06Data Retention

The App does not require a personal user account. Some devotional preferences and progress may be stored locally on your device to support app features.

Information processed by third-party analytics, diagnostics, advertising and hosting providers may be retained according to their policies and configured retention periods.

07Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can review the concern and take appropriate action.

Third-party SDK data processing and any applicable age-related advertising protections should be reviewed against the App's current Google Play audience settings.

08Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob. It also uses Supabase infrastructure to retrieve and deliver devotional content such as wallpapers and status images.

  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Supabase Privacy Policy

Google Play Billing disclosures should be added or reviewed when in-app purchases become available to users.

09Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to app features, service providers or legal requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

10Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hardeep.developers@gmail.com.
